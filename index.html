The 20 Amino Acids

structure · codes · etymology
Two ways to draw the same molecule, switchable in the Study toolbar. Skeletal is the abstract view — bonds as lines, carbons implied, backbone greyed out with an R marker where the side chain begins. Textbook is the explicit view — the backbone written out as ⁻OOC–Cα(–H)–NH₃⁺ with every atom of the side chain stacked above it and boxed in the class colour. Both show the free amino acid at pH 7; bond lengths and angles are identical across all twenty so shapes can be compared directly. Histidine is drawn as the neutral Nε2–H tautomer in skeletal style and as the protonated imidazolium in textbook style, matching each convention.
